# Desktop companion

Show the companion from Settings → General, the command palette, or the app
menu. It floats above normal desktop windows, independently of the chat view.
Point at the companion to reveal its drag handle and Greet button. Keyboard
focus reveals the same controls. Drag the handle to move it, or click the
capsule to open its activity tray.

The companion is an off-white and graphite capsule with shaded, articulated
parts. Quiet breathing and occasional glances leave pauses between gestures.
Its eyes follow your pointer in both directions. **Greet** raises an arm for a
wave. **Roll** and **Bounce** move its body and shadow together. Repeating a
gesture does not restart its blink or breathing.

Working has a focused expression and rotating core. Ready has smiling eyes;
Needs you has raised brows; Stopped has a concerned expression. The caption
reports activity, not verification of the agent's work. Attention and error
captions stay visible. Other captions appear on hover, keyboard focus, or while
the tray is open.
Choose **Greet**, **Roll** or **Bounce** for a reaction. Its caption still reports
workspace activity during play, and a missing status is labelled unavailable.
Choose a thread in the tray to open that conversation.

**Dance** starts a short routine with alternating steps and arm movements.
With **Idle gestures and celebrations** enabled, the companion stretches after
45 quiet seconds, yawns after 90 seconds, and rests after two minutes. Moving
your pointer onto it wakes it. It also dances when workspace status changes
from Working to Ready. These gestures stop while the tray is open, motion is
paused, or reduced motion is enabled. This setting stays on your device.
The companion never starts agent work on its own.

## Use voice

On macOS, open the tray and expand **Voice**. Press **Talk**, allow microphone
and speech recognition access if asked, and wait for **Microphone on** before
speaking. Say one command, such as "dance", "show my tasks", "pause motion",
"resume motion", or "read status". **Finish command** ends capture early;
otherwise listening stops after ten seconds. **Stop** cancels without acting.
Commands do not become agent prompts.

**Read status** speaks workspace counts without thread titles or message
contents. To hear status changes while the tray is open, select **Speak status
changes**. This option starts off whenever you reopen the tray. Closing the
tray stops voice input and output. Spoken updates are at least 15 seconds apart.

Recognition requires on-device US English support. If Talk is unavailable,
check English Dictation in macOS Keyboard settings, then reopen the tray.
If access is denied, check Microphone and Speech Recognition in Privacy &
Security settings. Capsule does not fall back to online recognition or save
audio and transcripts. Spoken output uses the system voice. Companion voice
is unavailable on Windows.

**Smaller** and **Larger** adjust the mascot. **Pause motion** stops its animations;
**Resume motion** restores them. These preferences stay on this Mac. Your system's
reduced-motion preference also disables animation. Hidden windows pause motion.
Escape closes the tray.

**Hide companion** removes it until you show it again. It initially appears
without taking keyboard focus, but clicking it allows keyboard interaction.
The companion is desktop-only; paired viewers cannot move or control it.
