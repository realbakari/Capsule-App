# Desktop companion

Show the companion from Settings → General, the command palette, or the app
menu. It floats above normal desktop windows, independently of the chat view.
Clicks on the empty space around it reach the window behind. Point at the
companion to reveal a small bar beside it with a drag handle and **Greet**.
Keyboard focus reveals the same bar. Drag the handle to move it; it comes back
to the same spot when you hide and show it again. Click the capsule to open its
activity tray.

The companion is an off-white and graphite capsule with arms and a face. It
breathes, floats, blinks and glances around at uneven moments, and its eyes
follow your pointer while it is over the companion. Each status has its own
pose, colour band, core symbol and face, so you can tell them apart from across
the screen:

- **Working**: a blue band, a turning ring in its core, focused eyes and busy
  arms.
- **Ready**: a green band, a check, smiling eyes and open arms.
- **Needs you**: an amber band, an exclamation mark, one hand raised, and a
  small hop every few seconds.
- **Stopped**: a red band, a cross, a worried face and a slumped stance.

When the status changes, the companion dips and springs into the new pose. The
caption reports activity, not verification of the agent's work. For Needs you,
Stopped and Ready it names the thread and stays visible; click it to open that
thread. Other captions appear on hover, keyboard focus, or while the tray is
open.

**Greet** waves. In the tray, **Roll**, **Bounce** and **Dance** play a
reaction. A reaction plays over the companion's breathing and pose, so
repeating one does not restart its blink or breathing. Its caption still
reports workspace activity during play, and a missing status is labelled
unavailable. The tray lists the five most urgent threads; choose one to open
that conversation.

With **Idle gestures and celebrations** enabled, the companion stretches after
45 quiet seconds, yawns after 90 seconds, and rests after two minutes. Moving
your pointer onto it wakes it. It also dances when workspace status changes
from Working to Ready. These gestures stop while the tray is open, motion is
paused, or reduced motion is enabled. This setting stays on your device.
The companion never starts agent work on its own.

## Use voice

On macOS, open the tray and expand **Voice**. Press **Talk**, allow microphone
and speech recognition access if asked, and wait for **Microphone on** before
speaking. Say one command, such as "dance", "show my tasks", "pause motion",
"resume motion", or "read status". **Finish command** ends capture early;
otherwise listening stops after ten seconds. **Stop** cancels without acting.
Commands do not become agent prompts.

**Read status** speaks workspace counts without thread titles or message
contents. To hear status changes while the tray is open, select **Speak status
changes**. This option starts off whenever you reopen the tray. Closing the
tray stops voice input and output. Spoken updates are at least 15 seconds apart.

Recognition requires on-device US English support. If Talk is unavailable,
check English Dictation in macOS Keyboard settings, then reopen the tray.
If access is denied, check Microphone and Speech Recognition in Privacy &
Security settings. Capsule does not fall back to online recognition or save
audio and transcripts. Spoken output uses the system voice. Companion voice
is unavailable on Windows.

**Smaller** and **Larger** adjust the mascot. **Pause motion** holds it still in
its current pose; **Resume motion** restores movement. These preferences stay
on this Mac. Your system's reduced-motion preference also holds it still. A
still companion keeps each status's pose, colour, symbol and caption. A hidden
companion freezes and carries on where it stopped. Escape closes the tray.

**Hide companion** removes it until you show it again. It initially appears
without taking keyboard focus, but clicking it allows keyboard interaction.
The companion is desktop-only; paired viewers cannot move or control it.

## On your phone

The desktop companion stays on your Mac. To follow your threads away from
the desk, pair the Capsule app for iPhone and iPad. See
[Capsule on iPhone and iPad](mobile-app.md).
